# Code Of Conduct

OT Math follows a simple standard: be clear, kind, and rigorous.

Participants are expected to:

- Treat contributors with respect.
- Explain technical disagreement with evidence.
- Welcome beginner questions.
- Avoid harassment, insults, and discriminatory language.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or limit participation when conduct harms the project community.
