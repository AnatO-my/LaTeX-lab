\section{WithArrows helpers}

Each annotation helper appears once below. The first two should be plain; the
last three should carry the documented bold prefixes.

\[
    \begin{WithArrows}
        2(x+3) &= 14
        \arrowcomment{expand the brackets} \\
        2x+6 &= 14
        \stepnote{subtract 6 from both sides} \\
        2x &= 8
        \steparrow{divide both sides by 2} \\
        x &= 4
        \reasonarrow{equal quantities have equal squares} \\
        x^2 &= 4^2
        \subarrow{\(4^2=16\)} \\
        x^2 &= 16
    \end{WithArrows}
\]
